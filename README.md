# AI Agent Adoption & Productivity Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes AI agent adoption and its impact on workplace productivity, employee job satisfaction, training, workload, and AI-related costs.

The analysis uses data related to AI usage across different industries, countries, company sizes, job roles, age groups, and AI skill levels.

The project combines **Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI** to perform data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and interactive dashboard development.

---

## Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze the level of AI adoption across different industries and countries.
- Understand productivity changes associated with AI usage.
- Analyze average hours saved through AI.
- Examine job satisfaction based on AI usage.
- Analyze AI training hours across different AI usage levels.
- Understand AI usage across company sizes and job roles.
- Analyze AI adoption by age group.
- Examine monthly AI-related costs.
- Understand workload levels by AI skill level.
- Identify patterns that can support better AI adoption strategies.

## Key Insights

The dashboard highlights several patterns in AI adoption and workplace productivity.

### AI Usage and Productivity

The analysis compares productivity changes across different AI usage levels and helps identify the relationship between AI adoption and workplace productivity.

### Time Savings

The dashboard shows average hours saved through AI usage and compares time savings across different AI usage categories.

### Job Satisfaction

Job satisfaction is analyzed according to AI usage levels to understand how AI adoption relates to employee workplace experience.

### Industry Adoption

Technology shows strong AI usage in the analyzed dataset, followed by industries such as Finance & Banking, Healthcare, Consulting & Professional Services, Retail & E-Commerce, and Manufacturing.

### Geographic Adoption

The dashboard compares AI usage across countries, including the United States, United Kingdom, Singapore, United Arab Emirates, and South Africa.

### Workforce Analysis

AI usage is also analyzed by age group, company size, and job role to understand where AI adoption is concentrated.
