# Project Insights

## 1. AI Adoption

The analysis examines AI adoption across different industries, countries, company sizes, job roles, and age groups.

## 2. Productivity

The dashboard analyzes the change in productivity associated with AI usage and compares productivity across different AI usage levels.

## 3. Time Saved

The project analyzes the number of hours saved per week through AI adoption and compares time savings across AI usage categories.

## 4. Job Satisfaction

The analysis examines job satisfaction in relation to AI usage to understand the relationship between AI adoption and employee experience.

## 5. AI Training

Training hours are analyzed across different levels of AI usage and AI skill levels to understand the importance of employee upskilling.

## 6. AI Costs

The dashboard examines monthly AI-related costs and compares costs across different workplace segments.

## 7. Industry Analysis

AI adoption is compared across industries to identify differences in adoption patterns.

## 8. Workforce Analysis

The project analyzes AI adoption across:

- Age groups
- Job roles
- Company sizes
- Countries
- Industries

## 9. Key Takeaway

The dashboard provides an interactive view of AI adoption and its relationship with workplace productivity, time savings, job satisfaction, training, and cost.

## Tools Used

- Excel
- Python
- SQL
- Power BI

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL querying, and Power BI visualization to convert AI adoption data into meaningful business insights.
